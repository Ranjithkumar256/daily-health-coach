Privacy Policy

App: Daily Health Coach 100% On-Device Storage

Last updated: September 11, 2026

1. Data Collection and Storage

Daily Health Coach is designed with privacy at its core. All personal health data, water intake logs, meals, workouts, body weight, and habit streaks are stored exclusively on your device using local device storage. None of your health or personal logs are uploaded to, stored on, or processed by external company servers.

2. Internet Connection and Advertisements

Our application uses Google AdMob to deliver advertisements so that the application remains 100% free for all users. An active internet connection is required to fetch and display ads from Google and its certified advertising partners.

Google AdMob may collect and process standard advertising identifiers (such as Google Advertising ID), device information, and coarse location data in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.

3. Children's Privacy

Our application does not knowingly collect any personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13.

4. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes.

5. Contact Us
