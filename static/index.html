🔒 100% On-Device Privacy Architecture

Daily Health Coach stores all your health telemetry, nutrition, weigh-ins, and habit logs strictly on your personal device. Your physical health information belongs exclusively to you and is never sold or harvested.

📋 Required App Permissions & Clear Reasons

To deliver full functionality while remaining completely free with unlimited access, we request the following permissions:

🌐 Internet & Network State (android.permission.INTERNET) Required

Why it is needed: Used to display Google AdMob banner & rewarded ads that keep Daily Health Coach 100% free with unlimited features unlocked. Also used if you connect your custom Cloud Database for remote backup.

📁 Phone File Manager & Storage Persistence

Why it is needed: Allows exporting your entire health database (.json) directly into your phone's File Manager (Downloads/Documents) so your streaks and health logs survive if you uninstall or reinstall the app, and lets you modify and re-import anytime.

⌚ Smartwatch & Fitness Sensors (Optional) Optional

Why it is needed: Enables streaming live pedometer step counts, resting heart rate, and active calorie burn from Wear OS, Apple Watch, or fitness trackers.
